# Kit requests (what `@onigam/lab-kit` 0.6.0 lacks, from the physics of Biodigester Lab)

Numbered, one paragraph each. Nothing here was worked around by patching the kit; each item says what the lab did instead.

1. **`sim/bio`, when a second lab needs it.** `src/sim/bio/` holds first-order decay, Arrhenius, Monod, the pH window (`kinetics.ts`), Buswell (`buswell.ts`), the carbonate and acetate buffer (`acidbase.ts`), a wet gas (`gas.ts`) and the two-population chain (`chain.ts`). No lab's name, nothing imported from outside the folder, nothing allocated in the hot path: `bio.test.ts` holds all three, and checks it against closed forms. It can move as it is, with its tests. The compost or dry-toilet labs of the roadmap may be its second user; until then it stays here, as `sim/thermal` stayed in the sand lab.
2. **The sun's path, in the kit.** The solar heater's `solar.ts` is not in the kit yet (`sim-thermal.md` says so), so `src/sim/climate.ts` has its own small declination, day's arc, clearness and diffuse share. A third copy is coming with the house lab. Asked: `sim/thermal`, or a `sim/sun`, with `sunTerms`, `horizontalFromDaily` and a diffuse share, which is what these three labs need.
3. **Speed labels for a lab that runs on a calendar.** The solar lab's request 3, still open. This lab runs at ×60 to ×86400, and a week's scenario at a day per second shows `×86400`, which says nothing to a visitor. `SPEEDS` passes five numbers.
4. **A strip value and a readout with a tag.** The solar lab's request 4. `StripValue` has `label`, `value`, `unit`. `stripValues` carries a `tag` of † or * that changes with the state: below about 25 °C, or anywhere but Dar es Salaam, a figure turns from a model result to an estimate. For now the lead can append it to the unit. Asked: `tag?: string` on `StripValue` and on `readout`.
5. **A long clock.** Mill Lab's request 8, the solar lab's 7. `stripClock` gives a day number and a month; the strip's `sub` is a plain string.
6. **Ice in `liquidWater`.** The material has no latent heat, and says so. A tank at Geneva touches 0 °C on some settings: `Plant` holds the node at zero and counts the heat it would have taken, which is enough here. A frost lab would need more. Not urgent.
7. **The camera on a section tool.** (3D role.) The `digester` world opens cut along its axis, and the section tool cuts it across at the overflow, best seen from above. `onCutaway` has no camera and `update` has none: the kit's open « camera in `update` » (mass 14, gravity 15) would let a world ask for a view. What the lab did: left the camera alone; the visitor orbits up. Selecting `rim-gap` opens that section by itself, from the angle the voice uses (request 14).
8. **A world opened on its section, or a label for what the tool does.** (3D role.) Mill's and the solar lab's request. Here the tool's name « Coupe » stands off on a world already cut. What the lab did: the tool cuts the other way, and the author's overview says so.
9. **A part category for what is alive, or categories a lab names.** (Data role; the solar lab's 6 asks for the second.) `PartCategory` has hydraulic, mechanical, electrical, structure, site. The drop's organisms are none of these. What the lab did: the organisms and the food particle are `site`, the stocks of acids, buffer, ammonium and the bubble `hydraulic`, and the sheet's label reads a little off for a methanogen. Asked: `'living'`, or a category the lab names.
10. **A `SceneDef` choice of the view during a guide step.** (Data role.) In `digester`, as long as a part of a guide is still to come the world shows the closed machine, because `now.hidden` selects `whole`: the inner feed pipe of step 4 is drawn inside a closed tank, and the visitor gets its outline at best. Not a kit fault. **Settled by the lead:** a guide's step now stays in section, by telling x-ray from a guide's wire preview in `partsNow`. Listed here only in case other labs want a guide to run on a world's opening view.
11. **An eyebrow that leaves a unit alone.** (Lead.) `.eyebrow` sets capitals, so a chart's title turns g/L into G/L and L/h into L/H, which are other units. What the lab did: the unit in a `span` with `text-transform:none` on the element, no CSS. Asked: a `unit` slot in the eyebrow, or no capitals inside a `small`.
12. **A label kept inside the view.** (Lead.) At 768 and 390 px, and on « every label » at 1280, a world's label that stands near the edge of the view is cut by it: `MÉTHANOGÈNES` in `slurry`, `MAISON ET CUISINE` in `yard`, `BRÛLEUR À BIOGAZ` in `gas-line`, `SUPPORT DE CASSEROLE` with every label on. Nothing is lost, the part is still there to click. Asked: the label layer flips a label to the other side of its dot when it would leave the view. What the lab did on QA M6: moved dots along their parts, five short names, 0 problems at each home in the three widths; with every label on, and with a phone's sheet raised, still open.
13. **The strip's values past the sixth.** (Lead.) This lab's strip has nine values, research's list; at 1,280 px and under the kit's CSS shows the first six, at 1,020 px the first four, and hides the rest with no way to reach them. What the lab did: left the order, and the panel's figures show the other three. Asked: a way to scroll them, or a lab's say in which six.
14. **The selection in a world's `update`.** (3D, QA M2.) The ring of `digester` can be seen only in the section across, and a click cannot ask for it: `ViewOpts` has no selection. What the lab did: `src/main.ts` hands the stage to `src/three/stage-link.ts` and the world reads `stage.selected`, as the solar lab reads its camera (its request 16). The tool's button does not light up for it. Asked: `selected` in `ViewOpts`, or a part's framing that can ask for the section, with the button following.
15. **What an overview holds beyond the parts.** (3D, QA M6; the solar lab's 17.) A narrow view fits the registered parts, so a name at the end of a long world runs out of it. What the lab did: the solar lab's `WorldRegistry`, an entry of no part. Asked: `overviewExtent` on `SceneModule`.
16. **A ghost for an alpha-tested card.** (3D, QA M4.) `GHOST` and `GHOST_WIRE` replace a mesh's material, so a leaf card loses its alpha and its whole quad draws. Thousands of 7 % quads stack to opaque. What the lab did: its leaf cards and crown masses hide unless solid. Asked: the registry leaves out, or lets a part mark, what has no place in a ghost.
17. **A clock that knows the guide is speaking.** (Lead, QA M1; the solar lab's 19.) At an hour a second the yard's sun set under the voice, which named 4 of the 7 parts of `scene-yard` in the dark. What the lab did: `src/voice-clock.ts`, a hook set on `lab.onFrame` from `src/main.ts`, since the hook needs `ctx.narration` and the store and `FrameInfo` carries neither. It holds a guide and a part's sheet too, and only in the world whose light follows the hour. Asked: `FrameInfo.ctx`, or a `Narration.onSegment(segment | null)` with the engine's own notion of the segment's start and end. Two labs have now written this by hand.
