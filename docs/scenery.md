**The tree's shade.** It stands in the north-west corner. A test takes the sun of the measured site, every month, through the 7.5 h of direct sun (K9), and holds the crown's shadow off the tank.

**The sun** is the simulation's: `sunAlt`, `sunAz`, +Z south, +X east. Moving the site or the month moves it. **The yard stays this yard at Geneva:** its plants do not change with the climate. A film or a sheet should not take it for Geneva.

**The family's traces, attached and resting** (`worlds.test.ts`): the cord leaves the bark where a ray of the test's own finds it, within 8 mm, and its two turns follow the bark measured all round (48 rays on the trunk's own triangles), not a circle; its other end on the pole's side; each cloth's top edge on the cord within its radius, every hem 0.9 m or more off the earth; the stool, the basin, the broom, the sandals and the pole on the earth within 3 mm (a leaning leg or rib raised by r·sin of its lean), on the plot's earth and clear of the pad and of the kitchen's wall; the plate's back on the basin's bead within 2 mm; the plate and the mug on the table, clear of the stand, the pot and the burner. The colours sober: faded cotton, timber, dull metal, white enamel; the kanga's print is generic. The cord casts no shadow: at this scale its shadow is a broken dotted line.

**The evening sky** (`yard/sky.ts`, film pass of 6/11). Two faults, seen on the film's stills: at 17:42 the sky stayed a hard day blue; from 17:30 to 18:00 Preetham's sky went about twelve times darker (its sun intensity falls with three's earth-shadow cut-off, the sky about as its square) while the yard, lit by the hemisphere, lost a tenth: a dark sky over a lit yard. Now: `skyLift` holds the low sky up by the inverse of that square from 9° down, so that it falls as `fall` says, a little faster than the ground's light, to about 0.6 of its 9° brightness at 18:00; a knee keeps the sun's glare from being lifted. `skyDusk` warms the sky near the horizon on the sun's side, in a band that stands higher towards the sun: the hue goes to amber and the brightness under a soft ceiling, blended by their logarithm (a linear blend left the bright haze white whatever its hue); the glare a few degrees round the sun stays white. Every weight is smooth; a fixed dither under one 8-bit step keeps the gradient from banding, the same on every frame. The day sky is untouched above 9°. **Open:** under the horizon Preetham's sky runs out at 2.3° down, and between about 1° and 2.3° down it still falls faster than the yard, before the night's own fade; the film's hour is well above that. Moving the cut-off to 6° down was tried: the 17:42 sky went pale lilac, and it was taken back.

**The plant is turned** on its pad so that its tap faces the kitchen and its funnel the yard (`PLANT_TURN`). The kitchen's wall is 3.5 m from the plant so that the reported 5 m of hose does reach it, falling to a low point at the wall's foot.

**Polish still open:** grass beyond the wall is flat in its colour at a distance; a neighbour's house from the air is a box with a roof. No part stands on them, and neither reads as wrong.
