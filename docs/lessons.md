The kit opens one scenario per lesson: the others are named word for word as the panel names them, so that all twelve can be reached from a lesson. Each lesson has four paragraphs of at most 95 words, a formula and three chips. A test keeps length from giving an answer away, in either direction, in each language: the right answer is the longest in 3 to 6 of the 14.

**Honest points.** Lesson 1 says the agreement on 289 L proves little: the kinetics leave the gas between 276 and 283 L†. Lesson 2 says the market waste is not retrieved, 64 to 76 L† against 126, why it cannot be, and draws no finding per kilo of solids. Lesson 3 says the brick doubles the pressure and not the flame, that the flow's 1.84 times against 1.4 is unexplained, that 1.06 and 1.04 m are one level. **Lesson 4 opens on what the model cannot decide,** with the slider, and every figure below 25 °C carries its star. Lesson 5 tells the failure as what appears by itself, says the model's 10 kg† is hopeful, and that its 35 kg† is not to be believed. Lesson 6 repeats no reassurance: it says what no figure exists for. Lesson 7 gives the leak's balance with each of its conditions, and the field's 4 of 12 with its second half.

**What the physics role asked not to write, and is not written:** leftovers losing to peelings per kilo of solids as a finding; half a brick doubling the flame; a cold tank stopping as a fact. The research's plan for lesson 2 asked for the first: the physics overrules it, and the lesson says why the thesis's 700 L settles nothing.
